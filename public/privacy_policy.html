4. Advertising & Tracking Choices

Advertisements allow us to offer Fruit Picker for free. Depending on your consent settings and region, you may see personalized (interest-based) or non-personalized (contextual) ads.

iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS 14.5 and newer, Fruit Picker presents the standard Apple App Tracking Transparency prompt asking for permission to track your activity across other apps and websites. If you select "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA is zeroed out and cannot be used for targeted advertising.

How to Manage Advertising Preferences on Your Device

  • On Android: Open Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads), and tap "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID".
  • On iOS: Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and manage tracking authorization for Fruit Picker or toggle off "Allow Apps to Request to Track".
