## 6. Advertising, Tracking & Your Choices

Fruit Picker displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Ads help keep the Game free to download and play.

### Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads
Depending on your region, age, and consent choices:
- **Personalized Ads:** Ads tailored to your interests based on previous browsing or app usage patterns, using your device's advertising identifier.
- **Non-Personalized Ads:** Ads that are contextual (related to the game itself or general geographical location such as city/country) rather than based on past behavior.

### iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT)
On iOS devices (iOS 14.5+), we request your explicit permission before accessing your Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", we will not access your IDFA for tracking purposes, and you will receive non-personalized ads.

### How to Opt Out of Targeted Advertising on Your Device
You can limit or reset ad tracking at any time through your mobile device settings:
- **On Android:** Go to `Settings` -> `Privacy` or `Google` -> `Ads` -> Tap **"Delete advertising ID"** or **"Reset advertising ID"**.
- **On iOS:** Go to `Settings` -> `Privacy & Security` -> `Tracking` -> Toggle off permission for Fruit Picker, or toggle off **"Allow Apps to Request to Track"**.
